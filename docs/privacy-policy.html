Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 13, 2026 • ScanFlow Photo

Key Summary: ScanFlow Photo is designed from the ground up as a 100% offline image utility. We do NOT collect, upload, store, or share any of your images, personal files, or personal identifiers.

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how ScanFlow Photo ("we", "our", or "the application") handles information when you use our mobile application. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it through uncompromising local-only processing.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

Because ScanFlow Photo functions entirely offline on your device, we do not collect any user information:

  • No Photos or Image Data: Any photo you select, compress, resize, crop, convert, or export is processed entirely in the local memory of your Android device. No photo ever leaves your phone.
  • No Biometric or Facial Data: Passport photo framing and guidance tools execute local pixel operations. No biometric templates or facial recognition data are created, stored, or transmitted.
  • No Analytics or Tracking: We do not include third-party tracking SDKs, device fingerprinting, or behavioral analytics.
  • No Location or Contact Data: We do not request or access your GPS location, contacts, or calendar.

3. Device Permissions & Scoped Storage

ScanFlow Photo requests only the minimum permissions required to perform local file operations:

  • Read Media / Storage: Used strictly to let you select images from your gallery or document picker to process.
  • Write Media / Storage: Used strictly to save the processed photos or generated PDFs to your device's storage (Pictures/ScanFlow).
  • Camera (Optional): If you choose to capture a new photo directly within passport or cropping tools, camera access is used exclusively for that capture session.

4. In-App Purchases & Google Play Billing

If you choose to purchase or subscribe to ScanFlow Pro, transactions are processed entirely through the official Google Play Billing system managed by Google LLC. We do not receive or store your credit card details, bank information, or billing address. Google Play's own Privacy Policy governs payment processing.

5. Children's Privacy

Our application does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. The application is completely safe for all audiences.

6. Security of Your Data

Your photos remain in your device's isolated application sandbox and standard Android scoped storage directories. We strongly protect your privacy by simply never transmitting your files over the internet.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

If we make any changes to this Privacy Policy, the revised policy will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
